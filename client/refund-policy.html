💸 Refund Policy

Your satisfaction is our priority.

Refund Eligibility

At ShopSphere, customers can request a refund if the product is damaged, defective, incorrect, or not delivered as expected.

To be eligible for a refund, the request must be submitted within 7 days of receiving the product.

Required Conditions

  • ✅ Product must be unused.
  • ✅ Original packaging should be available.
  • ✅ Invoice or Order ID is required.
  • ✅ Refund request must be made within the return period.

💳 Refund Process

  1. Submit a refund request from My Orders.
  2. Our team will review your request.
  3. If approved, pickup will be arranged (if required).
  4. After verification, your refund will be processed.
  5. The refund amount will be credited to your original payment method within 5–7 business days.

❌ Non-Refundable Items

  • Opened or used personal care products.
  • Gift cards and digital products.
  • Products damaged due to customer misuse.
  • Items returned without original packaging.
