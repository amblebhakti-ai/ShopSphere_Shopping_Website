🔄 Return & Replacement Policy

Easy Returns • Quick Replacement • Hassle Free Shopping

📦 Return Policy

At ShopSphere, customer satisfaction is our priority. If you are not satisfied with your purchase, you can request a return within 7 days of delivery.

  • ✅ Product must be unused and in its original condition.
  • ✅ Original packaging, tags and accessories should be included.
  • ✅ Return request should be raised within 7 days.
  • ✅ Refund will be processed after successful quality inspection.

🔁 Replacement Policy

If you receive a damaged, defective or wrong product, you can request a free replacement within 7 days of delivery.

  • ✅ Wrong Product Delivered
  • ✅ Damaged Product
  • ✅ Defective Product
  • ✅ Missing Accessories

📝 How to Return a Product?

Step 1

Login to your ShopSphere account.

Step 2

Open My Orders and select the product.

Step 3

Click on Return / Replace.

Step 4

Select the reason and submit your request.

Step 5

Our delivery partner will pick up the product.

Step 6

Your refund or replacement will be processed.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Can I return every product?

No. Some products like beauty products, personal care items and gift cards are non-returnable.

How many days does the refund take?

Refunds are usually processed within 5–7 business days after successful verification.

Is replacement free?

Yes. Replacement is free for eligible damaged or defective products.
